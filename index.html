Tangle Escape: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026. Publisher: Faelight Games, Imran Ali (silent.lips125@gmail.com).

Tangle Escape is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and the choices you have.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, hints, album stickers, themes and settings) is stored on your device only. We do not create accounts and we do not sell anything in the game.

Information collected by our partners

When you play with ads enabled, our partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and approximate location derived from your IP address, to deliver and measure ads.
  • Crash and performance data (device model, operating system version and what the game was doing when it crashed), to keep the game stable.

Partners: Google AdMob (ads and consent) and Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports).

Ads and your choices

Tangle Escape only shows reward ads that you choose to watch, for example to get an extra heart or double your coins. Where the law requires it (such as in the UK and the EU), the first time you open the game you can choose personalised ads or limited ads (not based on your interests). You can change this at any time under Settings > Privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

Tangle Escape is meant for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not run accounts, most data is held by our partners or on your device; contact us at silent.lips125@gmail.com and we will help. Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device.

Deleting your data

The game has no accounts, so there is nothing to delete on our side beyond what is on your device. To delete the data held on your device, clear the game's storage in your device settings or uninstall the game. To ask for data collected by our partners (advertising and crash reports) to be deleted, email silent.lips125@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Tangle Escape data deletion" and, if you can, your advertising ID (Settings > Google > Ads on Android). We will pass the request on and reply within 30 days.

Retention and security

Partner data is kept as long as each partner's own policy says. Connections use HTTPS.

Changes

We will update this page when the game changes and show the new date above.
